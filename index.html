    📘 Quick User Guide

    How to configure and use Bell in 3 simple steps:

    1. Assign Roles: Set one device to Partner 1 (e.g. desktop) and the other to Partner 2 (e.g. phone) in settings.

    2. Configure ntfy: Type the same secret topic name (e.g. bell-home-alert) on both devices. Install the free ntfy app on Partner 2's Android phone and subscribe to that same topic to receive loud lock-screen alerts.

    3. Sync via Google Sheet (Optional): Paste your Google Apps Script Web App URL on both devices to keep a shared history of bell rings and response times.
